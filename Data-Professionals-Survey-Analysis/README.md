# Data Professionals Survey Analysis | Power BI

## About the Project

I worked on this project to explore the experiences of people working in the data industry. The dataset contains survey responses from data professionals across different countries, covering areas such as salaries, job roles, programming languages, job satisfaction and career experiences.

My goal was to take the raw survey data, clean and transform it, and turn it into an interactive Power BI dashboard that makes the information easier to understand.

Rather than focusing only on the general overview of the survey, I also wanted to explore some of the other information hidden in the dataset and see what additional patterns and insights I could uncover.

## Tools Used

- **Power BI:** Data visualisation and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **Excel:** Working with the source dataset.

## Areas Explored

Some of the areas I explored in this project include:

- Salary differences across various data-related job roles.
- Programming languages commonly used by data professionals.
- Distribution of respondents across different countries.
- Satisfaction with salary and work-life balance.
- The difficulty people experience when trying to enter the data industry.

## Going Beyond the Basic Analysis

One thing I found interesting about this dataset was the amount of information it contained beyond the usual survey overview.

I wanted to look beyond the obvious figures and explore additional relationships in the responses. Some of the questions I considered included:

- Are professionals with different job titles experiencing similar levels of job satisfaction?
- Is there a noticeable relationship between salary satisfaction and work-life balance?
- How do salary patterns differ between roles within the data industry?
- Are there differences in career experiences across countries?
- What other patterns can be discovered by looking at the survey responses from different angles?

These additional areas of exploration helped me think more critically about the data and how to get more value from a dataset rather than simply creating visuals.
